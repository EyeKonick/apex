## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output, no UI framework, no client JS). Content lives in `src/data/*.json`.

## Users

Homeowners and small businesses in San Pedro City and nearby Calabarzon who need building, refurbishing or technical-system work (repairs, renovations, roofing, CCTV, radios, aircon, solar) and want one local contractor to inspect the site and quote it.

## Product Purpose

APEX Integrated Technical Services is a multi-trade technical and refurbishing contractor. The site exists to get visitors to request a free site inspection and quote. Success means a visitor taps "Get a Free Quote", calls, emails or messages on Facebook.

## Positioning

A single local team covering seven trades: Structural Engineering, House Renovation, Roofing, CCTV & Maintenance, Radio Communication, Ref & Aircon, and Solar Panel. You don't need to coordinate several contractors.

## Operating Context

Work happens on site in residential neighbourhoods around San Pedro City (Laguna, Calabarzon, PH): steel roof framing, CCTV and NVR setup, aircon units. Most leads arrive through Facebook and phone, so mobile is the primary context.
